01 Work

What we're building

We'd rather make a few things carefully than many things quickly. Everything below is built by the two of us, and everything is in active development.

  1. Kove

    In development iPhone

    A focus and app-blocking app. Set your own limits, shield the apps that pull you away, and let a quiet streak build behind you — with your screen-time data staying on your device.

    • Focus sessions
    • App blocking
    • Habits
    • On-device data
    Visit the Kove site →

  2. CoWatch

    In development iPhone & Android

    Keep track of the films, shows and anime you watch — built for two people sharing a couch rather than one person with a list. Shared watchlists that stay in sync, and it works perfectly well on your own.

    • Shared watchlists
    • Films & TV
    • Anime
    • iOS + Android

  3. Sail Suitely

    In development iPhone

    Luxury cruise discovery. Finds high-end sailings and filters by what actually matters at that end of the market — suite classes, the ship-within-a-ship enclaves, and the all-inclusive lines.

    • Suite classes
    • Ultra-luxury lines
    • Curated

  4. Bear Books

    In development Mac

    Small-business bookkeeping that stays out of your way. One ledger for everything, receipts you can scan straight in, and a one-click export your accountant will actually accept.

    • One ledger
    • Receipt scanning
    • Accountant export

  5. TouchPoint

    In development iPhone & Android

    A CRM for people who actually follow up. Scan a business card to add someone, set the next touchpoint, and let the app track who's due — your contacts stay on your device unless you choose to sync them.

    • Card scanning
    • Follow-ups
    • Local-first
    • iOS + Android
